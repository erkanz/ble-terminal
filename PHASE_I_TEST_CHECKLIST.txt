PURPOSE
-------
Harden the existing BLE/GATT/KISS/APRS application for release without rewriting or
weakening the qualified v13 RT-950 ownership/cache/CCCD behavior. This phase is about
lifecycle correctness, fault containment, compiler hygiene, sustained-use stability,
diagnostics, release documentation and final packaging.
