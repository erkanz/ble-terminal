BLE Serial Terminal v13 - GATT Inspector / RT-950 cache behavior
===============================================================

The Inspector no longer treats a second enumeration as the source of truth for the Auto Detect
service. Auto Detect is the owner of its live service/characteristic objects for the whole BLE
connection. Inspector borrows/reuses them and never disposes them.

For the cached Auto Detect service:
- GetCharacteristicsAsync is not repeated.
- all characteristics already discovered during Auto Detect are reused in the Inspector tree.
- selected write/notify characteristics are guaranteed to be present in the cache.
- Source=AUTO-DETECT/REUSED and Reused=Yes are visible.
- active main-terminal CCCD is reused rather than rewritten.

Other services are still enumerated normally, so Inspector remains a complete GATT browser as far
as Windows permits. Any AccessDenied result is logged with numeric GattCommunicationStatus and
protocol error when exposed.

The main terminal and Inspector share one SemaphoreSlim for discovery/read/write/CCCD operations.
ValueChanged notifications remain asynchronous and are not blocked by the operation gate.

Windows WinRT CCCD subscription uses:
WriteClientCharacteristicConfigurationDescriptorWithResultAsync(...)

The handler is attached before CCCD write. Only a Success result marks the receive data channel
ready. RT-950 raw FFE1 bytes are logged before being passed to the persistent KISS stream decoder.
